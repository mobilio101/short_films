- **딱 5분만.**: "할 일 앞에서 왜 갑자기 청소가 하고 싶어질까?" Putting off is about escaping a bad
  feeling now, not about time; the shadow offers relief, the hero opens the file for five minutes.
  Fits the core theme (미루기) and the example title in the rulebook. Sources:
  https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/91793/ (Sirois & Pychyl 2013, Social and Personality Psychology
  Compass 7(2):115-127); https://www.bu.edu/cas/files/2020/08/Why-you-procrastinate-PDF.pdf
- **폰은 다른 방에.**: "폰을 엎어 놓기만 해도 괜찮을까?" In experiments with about 800 people, a
  phone on the desk, even switched off, lowered test scores; a phone in another room did not. The
  shadow lives inside the phone glow; the hero carries it out of the room. Sources:
  https://news.utexas.edu/2017/06/26/the-mere-presence-of-your-smartphone-reduces-brain-power (Ward et
  al. 2017, J. Assoc. Consumer Research 2(2), doi 10.1086/691462);
  https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/keeping-your-smartphone-nearby-may-not-be-so-smart-2017080212163
- **하루 빠져도 괜찮아.**: "어제 운동 빼먹었으면, 이제 다 끝난 걸까?" UCL study: missing one day
  barely affected habit forming (only long gaps did), and habits took 18 to 254 days, not 21. The
  shadow says "망했어, 그냥 포기해"; the hero just does today. Topic: starting over. Sources:
  https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2009/aug/how-long-does-it-take-form-habit (Lally et al. 2010, European
  Journal of Social Psychology 40:998-1009); https://www.inverse.com/article/62104-how-to-stick-to-new-years-resolutions
- **오늘은 11시에 불 꺼.**: "졸린데 왜 폰을 못 내려놓을까?" Researchers named it bedtime
  procrastination: going to bed later than planned with nothing stopping you, linked to too little
  sleep. Night bedroom, phone glow, the shadow grows with each "한 편만 더". Keep it to sleep habits,
  no health claims. Sources: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00611/text
  (Kroese et al. 2014, Frontiers in Psychology 5:611);
  https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/bedtime-procrastination-introducing-a-new-area-of-procrastination/
